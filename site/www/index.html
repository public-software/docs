How we work

Spec-first cleanroom

Implement the standard, not the incumbent. Closed standards get a living spec and an executable conformance suite in specs.

RFCs for anything that crosses a repository

Interfaces are WIT packages in interfaces; changing one is a proposal with a decision log.

Signed off, signed, reviewed

DCO on every commit, signed commits and a linear history on main, one approving code-owner review; two in the platform ring.

Apache-2.0 OR MIT

Code under either licence; specs and content under CC-BY-4.0. No CLA. GitHub is a mirror of our own forge.
